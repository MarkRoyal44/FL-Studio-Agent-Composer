## How to get one

1. Pay **$PRICE** on Cash App to **[$Mark4949](https://cash.app/$Mark4949)**.
2. In the payment note, write: `Agent Composer commercial`, your company name, and how to reach you (an email or a Discord handle).
3. You'll get a license code within 48 hours.
4. In Agent Composer, go to **Settings › ♥ Support › Commercial license**, paste the code and click **Unlock**.
